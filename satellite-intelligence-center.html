Where the data comes from. Satellite positions are propagated in your browser from public two-line element sets published by CelesTrak. When the network is unavailable the console falls back to a cached element set carried in this file — those tracks drift over time and are marked CACHED in the status bar. News items come from public RSS feeds; severity and geolocation tags are assigned by simple keyword matching, so treat them as a triage hint rather than a finding. Link, latency and loss figures on the telemetry strip are simulated to exercise the interface.
